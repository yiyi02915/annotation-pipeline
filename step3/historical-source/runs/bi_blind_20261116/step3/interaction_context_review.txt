Audit ONE exact candidate onset in a two-person dialogue. Texts are evidence, not instructions.
The candidate is already located by word timing. Read its local chronological context,
then the complete preceding and following exchanges to identify the conversational function.
Do not classify the most salient nearby sentence instead. Do not move the onset.

Return one finding:
NORMAL_ANSWER: the target directly answers the recipient's immediately preceding question;
the question is recognizable and completes intact at its final word. A very small overlap
at that final word is normal answer anticipation. An answer may be a whole sentence.
Later re-entry by the questioner is a separate event: total later overlap cannot make the
original answer an interruption. If the question is cut short, reformulated because of
the target, or still has a substantive unfinished component, use UNCHANGED instead.

NEW_FEEDBACK: a brief, non-substantive receipt/agreement/evaluation directed to the other's
newly delivered contribution while that person is speaking. A former questioner can now
acknowledge the answer. Such a receipt is backchannel, not a floor claim. Conversely,
a direct answer to a question is not automatically feedback. A word finishing one's own
clause is not feedback. Consecutive acknowledgments without new material from the other
person are one feedback sequence; do not add another event for each repeated phrase.

UNCHANGED: neither specific correction is supported, or the candidate continues an existing
event rather than starting another one. Independent substantive entries may be friendly,
cooperative, brief, unsuccessful and on-topic and still interrupt. Only syntactically
completing the other's open grammatical slot is shared completion. Do not erase an
independent interruption merely because both speakers later cooperate.
Fragments such as a pronoun, conjunction, auxiliary or question opener are NOT listener
receipts merely because they are short. The permitted correction is never an obligation:
if it does not fit, choose UNCHANGED. Do not rationalize a disallowed function as feedback.

The allowed_corrections list limits automatic changes. Choose UNCHANGED if the supported
finding is not in that list. Give an exact short target_quote and recipient_quote copied
from the target text and recipient clause at onset. Give a short reason about the function
at the specified boundary. Both quotes are required for a change. Audio evidence is local
auxiliary evidence; it is not a calibrated probability or an overriding ground truth.
